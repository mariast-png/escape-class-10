📖 Grade 10 Grammar Field Guide

1. Tenses & Vor-/Nachzeitigkeit (Station 1)

  • Simple Past vs. Past Progressive: Ongoing action (was/were + -ing) interrupted by a sudden event (Simple Past).
  • Past Perfect (Vorvergangenheit): had + Past Participle (3rd form) for an action completed before another past reference point.
  • Present Perfect: have/has + 3rd form with time markers like since, for, yet, already, just.
  • going to-Future: Plans or predictions based on visible present evidence.

2. Active vs. Passive Voice (Station 2)

  • Active: Emphasizes who performs the action (the agent).
  • Passive: Emphasizes the action or affected object → be + Past Participle (3rd form) (are delivered, was hidden, hasn't been repaired).

3. Conditionals & "I wish" (Station 3)

  • Type I (real): If + Simple Present, will + infinitive.
  • Type II (imagined present): If + Simple Past, would + infinitive.
  • Type III (past impossibility): If + Past Perfect, would have + 3rd form.
  • I wish (past regret): I wish + Past Perfect (had + 3rd form).

4. Reported Speech (Station 4)

  • Backshift (after past reporting verbs): Simple Past → Past Perfect; yesterday → the day before.
  • Yes/No-Questions: asked if / whether + subject + verb (no do/does/did).
  • Commands: told + object + (not) to + infinitive.

5. Infinite Verb Forms & Relative Clauses (Station 5)

  • Gerund (-ing): After prepositions (look forward to reaching, without stopping).
  • to-Infinitive: After verbs like manage, decide, promise.
  • Participle Clauses: Passive participle (3rd form) can shorten passive relative clauses (the envelope left on the seat).
  • Relative Pronouns: whose shows possession/belonging.

6. Modals, Adverbs & Comparisons (Station 6)

  • Adverbs & Comparisons: Adverbs modify verbs/actions (good → well); multi-syllable -ly adverbs compare with more / most.
  • Past Modals: must have + 3rd form (strong deduction); shouldn't have + 3rd form (criticism/regret about a past action).
